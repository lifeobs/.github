# lifeobs

"lifeobs" stands for **Life observatories**.

We are a research team working on the ecology of polar and migratory species of birds, with particular emphasis on behavioural ecology and population dynamics.

We currently work on:

- Emperor Penguins and Adélie Penguins in Antarctica
- King Penguins in Sub-Antarctica.

We are mainly based at:

- [Centre d'Écologie Fonctionnelle et Évolutive (UMR5175), Montpellier, France](https://ror.org/008rywf59)
- [Centre Scientifique de Monaco (CSM), Monaco](https://ror.org/04kptf457)

## What you will find here

- **Article scripts**: the code needed to reproduce the analyses of our publications.
  [Browse them](https://github.com/orgs/lifeobs/repositories?q=topic%3Aarticle-scripts).
- **Software**: tools developed and used by the team. Some repositories are currently private.
- **Wiki and website**: under construction.

## Open and reproducible research

Our code (and, when relevant, our data) is archived with DOIs in the
[lifeobs community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/lifeobs/).
Datasets may also be deposited in domain repositories such as the Movebank Data Repository.
New article repositories follow a common structure, based on our template:
[template-article-scripts](https://github.com/lifeobs/template-article-scripts).

Our code is released under open licenses (usually GPL-3.0-or-later): check the `LICENSE` file of each repository.

## How to cite

Please cite the article associated with a repository, and the Zenodo archive of the code
when you reuse it. Each repository has a `CITATION.cff` file and a "Cite this repository"
button on GitHub once it is published.

## For team members

The [admin guide](https://github.com/lifeobs/.github/blob/main/ADMIN_GUIDE.md) describes
how repositories are named, organized and archived.
To start a new article repository, contact an owner of the organization.
